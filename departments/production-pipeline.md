# Production Pipeline

Content moves through three departments in one flow, without leaving the
system: writing, visuals, publishing.

```
Copywriting ──► Creative Production ──► Platform Operations
   words            visuals                publishing
```

## 1. Copywriting — the words

- Input: a brief and a source the human approved.
- Output: draft, rewrite, tone check — for a post, a script, a caption,
  or a deck.
- Rules: no invented facts, clients, or numbers. If a claim has no
  source, it does not go in.

## 2. Creative Production — the visuals

- Input: the finished copy from Copywriting, plus a visual brief.
- Output: images, video, or a deck — brief, draft, review, final file.
- Rules: owns visual assets end to end, but does not decide what gets
  published. The publish decision stays with the human and with
  Platform Operations.

## 3. Platform Operations — publishing and follow-up

- Input: finished pieces from Copywriting and Creative Production.
- Output: a per-platform checklist (format, size, caption, timing),
  the posting record, and the follow-up numbers.
- Rules: does not write copy and does not make visuals. It publishes
  what it receives and reports back how it performed.

## Handoffs

Each handoff between two departments is a written handoff, not a verbal
one: the piece itself, the brief it was made against, and the checklist
for the next step. A department that receives an incomplete handoff sends
it back instead of guessing.
